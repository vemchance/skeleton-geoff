## The Celtic Cross

Ten cards. Lay them in number order. Card 2 is laid across card 1. Cards 7 to 10 form the staff, read from the bottom up.

1 is the present. The heart of the matter; where you are now.
2 is what crosses you. The challenge or obstacle in the way.
3 is the foundation (below). The root of the situation; what lies underneath it.
4 is the recent past (left). What has just happened or is passing.
5 is the crown (above). Your conscious aim; the best that can be achieved.
6 is the near future (right). What is coming soon.
7 is yourself. Your attitude and how you see yourself in this.
8 is external influences. Other people and your surroundings.
9 is hopes and fears. What you hope for or dread, often both at once.
10 is the outcome. Where things are heading if nothing changes.

## The Safe Passage

Seven cards. Lay them in number order. Cards 2 to 6 sit around the centre like the points of a star. Card 7 is laid across card 1.

1 is the Traveller (centre). You, and what you bring to the situation.
2 is the Quest (top). What is missing; the reason for the journey and what is at stake.
3 is the Path Behind (upper left). Wounds suffered and lessons learned.
4 is the Path Ahead (upper right). Room for growth and discovery.
5 is the Obstacles (lower left). The challenges you will face.
6 is the Windfall (lower right). What you gain once the obstacles are faced.
7 is the Destination (across 1). Where the journey ends.

## The Three-Card Spread

Three cards. Lay them in number order, left to right. Choose one of the three readings below before you draw.

### Past, Present, Future
1 is the past. What has led to this situation; influences that are behind you.
2 is the present. Where things stand now; what is most important at the moment.
3 is the future. Where things are heading if they carry on as they are.

### Situation, Obstacle, Guidance
1 is the situation. What is going on; the circumstances as they stand.
2 is the obstacle. What is in the way or making things difficult.
3 is the guidance. Advice on how to approach it.

### Release, Embody, Action
1 is what to release. What to let go of.
2 is what to embody. A quality to take on or bring more of into yourself.
3 is the action. A step to take next.
